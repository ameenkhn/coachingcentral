1:1 Business Strategy Session

Help me figure it out

Book a Strategy Session

A focused one-to-one session that looks at your qualifications and experience, interests, strengths, niche, audience, positioning, budget and platform — and turns it into a practical 30–90 day roadmap.

  • Assess — where you are, what you've got to work with, and what's realistic.
  • Roadmap — a practical 30–90 day plan you can actually act on.
  • Decide — whether you do it yourself, get ongoing support, or hand over implementation.
Flexible engagement

Ongoing Support

Stay with me

Talk About Ongoing Support

For coaches who want someone experienced alongside them as the business takes shape. Engagements are flexible — weekly, bi-weekly, bi-monthly or as required — and shaped around what you're working on.

  • Marketing guidance
  • Social profile reviews
  • Digital presence review
  • Strategic reviews
  • Direction changes
  • Performance reviews
  • Business guidance

Support is scoped after a Strategy Session, so it fits what your business actually needs.
