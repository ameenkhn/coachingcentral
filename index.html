The Coaching Central Method

There’s more to building a coaching business than learning how to coach.

That gap — the part after the certificate — is exactly what Coaching Central exists for. It’s one continuous journey with three stages, and where you are decides where you start.

Tap a stage to see what it covers.

Stage 01 · Build

Decide what you’re building and who it’s for — then how you should be known for it. Niche, audience, positioning, offer.

Put the right message in the right places so the right people find you. Presence, content, platforms, leads.

Look at what’s working, improve what isn’t, and change direction when the business needs to. Measure, optimise, pivot, scale.

Build. Attract. Grow. Repeat.

It’s a cycle, not a ladder. Growth often takes you back into Build — when your positioning sharpens, your offer changes, or your audience shifts.

The experience behind Coaching Central

The same question, again and again: “I’m certified. Now what?”

Coaching Central grew out of 14+ years of working with, managing and promoting coaches across India — and hearing that question again and again from people who were good at coaching and stuck on everything around it.

It isn’t a certification body, a course marketplace or an agency. It’s the practical, business-side help coaches kept asking for — open to every discipline, independent of any school, with expertise in the background and mentorship in the foreground.
