Who it's for

For the coach who has the certificate — and a blank page.

You've finished training and you're ready to start. But the practice needs structure before it needs marketing: how sessions are prepared, how clients are onboarded, how follow-ups and assignments work, what "launched" actually means. The Launch Kit gives you that structure so you can start working with clients properly, from the first one.
