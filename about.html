The experience behind Coaching Central

We've been helping coaches figure this out for 14 years.

Coaching Central grew out of 14+ years of working with coaches across India — helping people move from the uncertainty after training to clarity about who they serve, what they're known for, where they show up, what they invest in, and what they do next.

A business-growth ecosystem for coaches — not a course, and not an agency.

Coaching Central is for coaches who know how to coach, but need help figuring out how to position, present and grow themselves in the market. The immediate focus is newly certified coaches; the door is open to life coaches, NLP practitioners and master practitioners, relationship coaches, parenting coaches, executive coaches and every other coaching professional.

  • Independent. Not tied to any certification body or school.
  • Open to all coaches. Whatever discipline you trained in, the business questions are the same.
  • Expertise in the background, mentorship in the foreground. Practical, warm and direct.

What we believe

Don't tell coaches what you sell. Help them see their next step.

Everything here is built so a coach can understand where they are, what they need next, and how Coaching Central can help them get there — whether that's a free template or hands-on implementation.

  • Where you are

    Just certified, starting out, building seriously or ready to grow — the right next step depends on it.

  • Build · Attract · Grow

    One continuous, cyclical method — not three static boxes. Growth often takes you back into Build.

  • Your level of involvement

    Do it yourself, get guidance, keep us alongside you, or have us build it. Never only the biggest option.
