The experience behind Coaching Central

We’ve been helping coaches answer “now what?” for 14 years.

Coaching Central grew out of 14+ years of working with, managing and promoting coaches across India — helping people move from the uncertainty after training to clarity about who they serve, what they’re known for, where they show up, what they invest in, and what they do next.

Where it came from

The same conversation, over and over: a good coach, stuck on everything around the coaching.

Over fourteen years of working with coaches — managing them, promoting them, helping them get seen — one pattern kept repeating. The training was good. The coaching was good. And then the certificate went on the wall and the questions started: who do I help, what am I known for, where do I find people, what do I spend on, what do I do first.

Nobody had covered that part. Certification bodies teach coaching. Course marketplaces sell more courses. Agencies sell marketing. None of them sit with a newly certified coach and help them make the handful of decisions that everything else depends on.

Coaching Central was built to be that place: practical, business-side help for coaches — from the first decision about who you serve to the point where you’re growing something that works. Expertise in the background. Mentorship in the foreground.

What it is

A business-growth ecosystem for coaches — not a certification, a course marketplace, or an agency.

For aspiring, newly certified and growing coaches who want to turn their expertise into a working business — with practical kits, 1:1 strategy, services, a community and short, useful learning. Newly certified coaches first; the door is open to every coaching discipline.

  • Independent. Not tied to any certification body or school. Life, NLP, executive, relationship, parenting — the business questions are the same.
  • A journey, not a catalogue. Everything sits on Build → Attract → Grow, and where you are decides where you start. The method
  • You choose how much help you want. Do it yourself, work it out with us, or let us help build it. Never only the biggest option.
  • No promises we can’t keep. We don’t promise clients, income or outcomes. We help you make the next decision well, and the one after that.

What we believe

Don’t tell coaches what you sell. Help them see their next step.

Everything here is built so a coach can understand where they are, what they need next, and how Coaching Central can help them get there — whether that’s a kit or hands-on implementation. There is always a way forward.

  • Where you are

    Just certified, starting out, building seriously or ready to grow — the right next step depends on it.

  • Build · Attract · Grow

    One continuous, cyclical journey — not three static boxes. Growth often takes you back into Build.

  • Your level of involvement

    Do it yourself, work it out with us, or let us help build it. You choose how much help you want.
