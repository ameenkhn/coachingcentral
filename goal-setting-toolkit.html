The journey

Six questions that turn a goal into a process.

Most goal-setting stops at "define it well". The toolkit keeps going — through the why, the definition, the action, the tracking and the part everyone skips: how to keep moving when momentum drops.

  1. What do I want?Get underneath the vague version to the real one.
  2. Why do I want it?The reason that survives a bad week.
  3. How do I define it?Specific, measurable — and meaningful.
  4. How do I act on it?The next concrete step, not the whole mountain.
  5. How do I track it?Evidence of progress a client can see.
  6. How do I keep moving?What to do when motivation isn't there.
Who it's for

Coaches who want sharper sessions and clients who make visible progress.

The toolkit is deliberately separate from the business-building ecosystem. It's for the coaching itself — a structure you can use with clients from the next session onwards, whatever your discipline.

  • Use it in sessions — walk clients through the six questions.
  • Use it between sessions — pair it with assignments and follow-ups.
  • Use it on yourself — your own business goals need the same rigour.
