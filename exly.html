Why Exly

One platform for the whole business side of coaching.

Most coaches end up stitching together a payment link, a form, a calendar, a WhatsApp group and a website. Exly replaces the stitching — which is exactly why Coaching Central runs on it.

Sell 1:1 sessions

Appointments with your availability, booking and payment handled for you.

Run workshops & webinars

Live sessions and group classes — single events or multi-week cohorts.

Courses & recorded content

Package what you teach into courses and recordings people can buy any time.

Community & memberships

Paid communities and memberships — the Coaching Central Community itself is one.

Payments & invoicing

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets — with invoices and confirmations sent automatically.

Website, app, CRM & marketing

A no-code website, a brand-labelled mobile app, an inbuilt CRM, offers and promo codes, and SEO — as listed on exlyapp.com.

What it means for you

The platform and the set-up — sorted.

Technology is one of the areas we handle in Services. With Exly, it’s the one we can hand you fastest.

  • We run on it

    The Coaching Central Community, workshops and sessions are hosted, booked and paid for on Exly. We use what we recommend.

  • We set you up on it

    Offers, pages, payments, tracking — done with you or for you as part of Services, after a Strategy Session decides what you actually need.

  • We keep it simple

    One platform instead of five tools. You spend your time coaching, not administering — and you can see what’s working.

Where the platform fits
